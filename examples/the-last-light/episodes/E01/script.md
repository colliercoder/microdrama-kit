# E01 - "The Night the Light Went Out"

Arc: the old keeper is gone -> the smallest one is left -> the storm -> the lamp dies -> he climbs.
Cliffhanger: the lens is dark and something huge is moving out on the water.

## Shot 1
visual: Old Moss sits in a worn armchair beside a crackling fireplace, spectacles on, looking straight into the camera, calm and kind.
location: cottage
characters: moss@old
camera: locked-off medium close-up, a very slow push-in
motion: the fire flickers, Moss blinks once and breathes, his mouth stays closed
narration: [warm] Everyone on the island says I was too small for the job. [chuckles softly] They were right.

## Shot 2
visual: Young Moss in his oversized yellow raincoat stands at the bottom of a spiral stone staircase that climbs up out of frame, holding a brass key in both paws.
location: lamp_room
characters: moss@young
camera: slow tilt up from Moss to the endless stairs above him
motion: Moss looks up and tightens his grip on the key, rain sounds implied by drips on the stone
narration: [quietly] The old keeper left me one brass key... [wistful] and two hundred stairs.

## Shot 3
visual: Young Moss stands upright beside the great brass lens in the lamp room, the lens many times his height, rain streaming down the windows behind him.
location: lamp_room
characters: moss@young
camera: slow arc left around Moss and the lens
motion: the lens turns slowly and its warm light sweeps across Moss
narration: [proud] Every night I climbed them. [determined] Every night, the light went round.

## Shot 4
visual: A huge storm wave breaks against the black rocks at the foot of the lighthouse, the beam cutting through the rain above.
location: shore
characters:
camera: low angle, locked-off, the wave rising toward camera
motion: the wave surges up the rocks and falls back, spray drifting
narration: [tense] Then came the storm the old ones still whisper about.

## Shot 5
visual: The lamp room goes dark: the brass lens is unlit, young Moss stands upright in front of it, lit only by a cold flash of lightning, his eyes wide.
location: lamp_room
characters: moss@young
camera: slow push-in on Moss
motion: a single lightning flash fades, Moss takes one small step back, his raincoat drips
narration: [hushed] And at midnight... [shocked] the light went out.

## Shot 6
visual: From the dark lamp room window, a vast shadow moves across the stormy sea far below, small Moss in the corner of the frame pressed to the glass.
location: lamp_room
characters: moss@young
camera: slow push-in past Moss toward the dark water
motion: the shadow glides slowly under the waves, lightning flickers once
narration: [whispering] Because out there, in the dark... [ominous] something had been waiting for it to go out.
