# E01 - "The Runt"

Arc: the bet -> the first shift -> still standing -> one nod of respect -> the mountain groans.
Cliffhanger: every dog goes silent; next episode answers what the groan was.

## Shot 1
visual: The old bulldog narrator sits at a desk in a dark room, a warm lamp to one side, looking straight into the camera, calm and steady.
location: narrator_room
characters: hero@present
camera: locked-off medium close-up, a very slow push-in
motion: he blinks once and breathes, his mouth stays closed, nothing else moves
narration: [gravelly] They bet a week's pay I'd quit... [low] before the sun went down.

## Shot 2
visual: A tiny bulldog pup in an oversized jacket and yellow hard hat stands upright in a mine cage, surrounded by grown bulldog miners twice his size, all headlamps on.
location: pit
characters: hero@pup, crew@pit
camera: slow tilt down from the miners' faces to the pup
motion: the cage sways gently, headlamp beams swing, everyone stays upright
narration: [weary] First shift in the hole. Just a pup... [ominous] dropped a thousand feet into the dark.

## Shot 3
visual: The pup stands upright at a wall of tools, both paws wrapped around the handle of the biggest flat-blade shovel, the blade taller than he is.
location: pit
characters: hero@pup
camera: low angle, slow push-in on the pup
motion: he lifts the shovel off its hook and plants it, staying upright on two legs
narration: [proud] So I grabbed the biggest shovel on the wall... [driven] and I dug like the mountain owed me money.

## Shot 4
visual: The pup, black with coal dust from head to toe, still standing upright at the end of the shift, shovel in his paws, the crew walking past him on the left.
location: pit
characters: hero@pup, crew@pit
camera: locked-off medium shot
motion: miners file past left to right, the pup does not move, dust drifts in the headlamp beams
narration: [quietly] By the end of the shift... [proud] I was still standing.

## Shot 5
visual: The oldest bulldog miner, grey muzzle, stops in front of the pup and gives one slow nod.
location: pit
characters: crew@pit, hero@pup
camera: over-the-shoulder from behind the pup, holding on the old miner
motion: the old miner nods once and holds the pose, nobody talks
narration: [warm] The oldest hound in that crew... [hushed] he just nodded. Once.

## Shot 6
visual: Deep inside the tunnel, a fine trickle of dust sifts down from the rock roof between two timber supports.
location: pit
characters:
camera: slow push-in toward the trickle of dust
motion: dust falls gently, a timber support creaks slightly, calm and controlled
narration: [hopeful] I thought I'd won. [ominous] Then, deep inby... the rock started to groan.

## Shot 7
visual: The whole crew and the pup frozen mid-step, headlamps turned toward the dark end of the tunnel, no one moving.
location: pit
characters: hero@pup, crew@pit
camera: very slow push-in toward the dark
motion: nobody moves, a single headlamp flickers, everyone stays upright
narration: [whispering] And every dog in that hole... went dead silent.
